## Overview
The Aeolarium is a steam-powered ship designed to carry endangered species and research animals across the galaxy to Reynard Wildlife Sanctuary. Initially crewed by a team of three, Vesper is the only one who remains after an attack from another planet. Now alone onboard, they must continue their mission to transport these animals to safety while maintaining the ship’s power and taking care of their own well being.

## Gameplay
Keep watch on the status bars.  The less content the animals are, the more likely they are to make an escape.  Head to the kitchen, and click on the counter to interact and collect food (only one item at a time), then click on the wall to leave the kitchen view.  Head to the animal containment room, and click on the designated enclosure to feed them.  

Resources used for the animals drains power from the ship.  Head to the back of the ship, and click on the plate in front of the boiler to change the light settings.  Brighter lights drain more power.  The ship slowly regenerates power as it moves.
